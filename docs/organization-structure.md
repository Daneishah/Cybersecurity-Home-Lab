# Sew4U Academy Organization Structure

## Organization

Sew4U Academy is modeled in this lab as a growing nonprofit organization with multiple programs, staff members, contractors, volunteers, and operational departments.

The cybersecurity home lab is designed to simulate the technology and security needs of a real organization supporting substantial program activity, revenue, grant funding, employees, and community services.

## Departments

- Executive / Administration
- Human Resources
- Finance & Grants
- Programs & Youth Services
- Sewing / Creative Arts
- It's Healthy Baby! / Wellness
- Operations / Facilities
- IT & Cybersecurity
- Marketing / Community Outreach
- Volunteers / Contractors

## Security Goals

The lab will apply role-based access, least privilege, account lifecycle management, security monitoring, and other cybersecurity controls based on each department's responsibilities.

## Planned Scenarios

- New employee onboarding
- Employee termination and account deprovisioning
- Department transfers
- Password resets and locked accounts
- Incorrect permissions
- Privileged account management
- Volunteer and contractor access restrictions
- Suspicious login investigations
- Phishing incidents
- Lost or stolen devices
- Security audits
- Incident response
